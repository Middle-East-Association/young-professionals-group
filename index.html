About the Group

The Middle East Association (MEA) has established the Young Professionals Group (YPG), a network designed to foster meaningful connections and support the professional growth of the next generation of UK–MENA leaders.

Membership is free to join, offering exclusive invitations to our events. By signing up, you'll become part of our mailing list, ensuring you receive updates and invitations to upcoming activities and opportunities.

Please note

Signing up is completely free. The YPG is open to individuals under 35, and all applications to join are reviewed. Membership is granted on approval.

Established 1961

About the MEA

The Middle East Association (MEA) connects the UK with the Middle East and North Africa, fostering partnerships across vital sectors and promoting collaboration in trade and investment, culture and education, and development.

Governance

Connecting government entities and policy makers across the region.

Business & NGOs

Bridging commercial and non-profit organisations.

Academia

Linking educational institutions and researchers.
